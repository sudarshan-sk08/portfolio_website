Overview

One sled, every electrical interface

SPINE is the physical structure that carries MHPR's flight computer, sensors and wiring inside the airframe. Rather than fixing boards directly to the airframe wall, everything mounts to a single removable sled — so the whole avionics stack can be pulled, inspected, rewired and reinstalled as one unit instead of disassembling the rocket around it.
