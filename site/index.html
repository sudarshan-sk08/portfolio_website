Featured project

Big Unilateral Sear Spring System (BUSSS)

Mechanical Recovery System - Monash High Powered Rocketry

MHPR's first fully mechanical, non-pyrotechnic parachute deployment system. A linear-actuator-triggered sear and spring release vents a CO2-pressurised piston to separate the airframe - replacing black-powder charges entirely. Sized through iterative FEA and flight-validated at 10,000 ft AGL.
